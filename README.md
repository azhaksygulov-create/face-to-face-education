# face-to-face-education
# 🎓 The Impact of Social Interaction on Online Learning

## 📌 About the Project

This project presents an academic study about the role of social interaction in synchronous online education.

The project is based on the article **“The Impact of Social Interaction on Students’ Satisfaction with Synchronous Online Learning at FLDM”** by Jihane El Moussaddar and colleagues, published in *Social Sciences & Humanities Open* in 2025.

The main topic of the project is the **lack of face-to-face interaction in online education settings**.

---

## 🎯 Research Purpose

The study examines how social interaction affects students’ satisfaction with synchronous online learning.

It focuses on two main types of interaction:

* 👨‍🏫 Instructor–student interaction
* 👥 Student–student interaction

---

## 🔬 Methodology

The research involved **52 graduate students** from Sidi Mohamed Ben Abdellah University in Fez, Morocco.

The researchers used a **mixed-methods approach**, combining:

* Online questionnaires
* Semi-structured interviews
* SPSS statistical analysis
* Pearson correlation
* Qualitative coding and thematic analysis

The interviews involved **20 students**.

---

## 📊 Key Findings

### 1. Instructor–Student Interaction

Better communication with instructors was associated with higher student satisfaction.

Students valued responsive teachers and opportunities to participate.

### 2. Student–Student Interaction

Communication and cooperation between students improved the online learning experience.

Informal interaction helped students feel more connected.

### 3. Sense of Community

A stronger sense of community was linked to greater satisfaction.

The study reported:

**r = 0.701, p < 0.001**

This indicates a strong positive relationship between the sense of community and students’ satisfaction.

---

## ✅ Advantages of Online Learning

The study identified several benefits of online education:

* Flexibility
* Convenience
* Studying from home
* Online communication tools
* Easier access without being physically present

---

## ⚠️ Challenges

Students also reported several difficulties:

* Lack of interaction
* Feelings of isolation
* Distractions
* Technical problems
* Poor internet or audio quality

Many students preferred **face-to-face or blended learning**.

---

## 💡 Main Idea

> **Online learning is not only about technology — social connection also matters.**

Technology can provide access to education, but effective communication between teachers and students and between students themselves remains an important part of the learning experience.

---

## 🚀 Recommendation

A balanced approach can combine the flexibility of online learning with the social benefits of face-to-face interaction.

Possible solutions include:

* Live discussions
* Group activities
* Student collaboration
* Regular teacher feedback
* Blended learning

---

## 👨‍💻 Project Relevance

This topic is especially relevant to information technology because modern digital systems increasingly support remote learning, communication, and collaboration.

Understanding how people interact online can help developers and educators create more effective digital learning environments.
